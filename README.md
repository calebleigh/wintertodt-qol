# Wintertodt QoL

A darker, stormier Wintertodt in your own colors, with a few gameplay cues to keep you warm.

Everything is client-side and only you see it.

## Arena

- **Darken and tint the area** around the Wintertodt and the camp in one color. It fades out gradually around the camp, with no hard line against the white snow.
- **Storm overlay**: a dim, darker edges, and snow blowing across the screen while a round is on. The snow eases in when a round starts and eases off when it ends.

## Wintertodt

- **Recolor the Wintertodt** with a main color and two accents.
- **Recolor snow attacks** and outline the falling snow so it's easy to spot. The outline strength is adjustable.

## Fire

- **Recolor the fire**: the brazier flames, the magic fire and the door glow.
- **Recolor supplies**: the roots, kindling and other Wintertodt items in your inventory.

With 117 HD, the glows won't change color. 117 HD adds its own lights that plugins can't change.

## Gameplay help

- Outlines on **downed Pyromancers** and **broken or unlit braziers**.
- **Missing supplies**: outlines the crates for the tools you're missing (knife, hammer, axe, tinderbox), the potion crate when you have no potions, and the roots when you have nothing to burn.
- **Points panel**: activity, round points, inventory points, kindling and the next round timer.
- **Round start notification**, a set number of seconds before a round starts.
- **Low warmth**: a glow around the edge of the screen when your warmth drops below a set level.
- **Potion ready**: highlights rejuvenation potions when a full dose wouldn't be wasted.

If you use RuneLite's built-in Wintertodt plugin too, turn off its overlay and set its round notification to 0 so you don't get two of each.

## Fun

- **Random colors each round**: new colors for the Wintertodt and the fire after every round.
- **Skiing**: stand, walk and run on skis around the Wintertodt.
- **Cold breath**: puffs of frosty breath from everyone's mouths. Yours come faster as your warmth drops.
